2. In short

  • No account is needed; we do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • The app has no server of its own. Your progress is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.
  • The app contains no analytics.
  • When advertising and in-app purchases are enabled, the third parties listed in Section 4 may process data.

3. Information stored on your device

Game progress (chapter, score, streak, review list, learned words), an unfinished puzzle, coin balance and hint charges, settings (sound, music), whether the ad-free version was purchased and a daily reward counter. We cannot access this information. It is deleted when you delete the app or use Settings → “Reset progress”. On iPhone it is kept in the storage iOS assigns to the app and may be included in your device backup.

4. Third parties

Apple (App Store and in-app purchases)

Downloads and in-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details; the device only receives confirmation that a purchase succeeded. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob) [TO BE FINALISED BEFORE ADS ARE ENABLED]

Users may choose to watch rewarded ads to earn coins; a rewarded ad is shown only when the user starts it, and this option stays available to owners of the ad-free version. Ads the user did not start (interstitials) are not shown in the ad-free version. Ads are provided by Google. Non-personalised ads are requested and App Tracking Transparency permission is not requested. To serve ads and operate its service, Google may process: IP address (to estimate approximate location), device identifiers, ads shown and interactions with them, usage information such as app launches, and crash and performance data. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Pronunciation

The speaker button reads words aloud with your device’s speech engine. On iPhone this happens on the device. Some web browsers may use their own online speech service.

Web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep technical logs such as IP addresses. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
